Le Mahjong Club

Support for the club's iPhone app

Le Mahjong Club is the house reference for our Hong Kong Mahjong club: the payout calculator and cash scale, the house rules with example hands, a seat-wind marker for the table, and a guide to the characters on the tiles.

Getting the app

The app is distributed to club members through Apple's TestFlight. Ask the club organizer for the invitation link, install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the link on your iPhone.

Questions and problems

Found a scoring mistake, a rule that reads wrong, or something hard to see at the table? Tell the club organizer in person or by email at colinwong@gmail.com. TestFlight testers can also send feedback from inside the TestFlight app.

Common questions

The app stopped opening. TestFlight builds expire 90 days after they are uploaded. Open TestFlight and install the latest build.

The winds are in the wrong seats. On the cover, tap 風 to show the seat winds, then tap the rotate button beside East until East faces the dealer. Tap the centre to change the table wind.

Does it need an account or the internet? No. Everything is on the phone.
