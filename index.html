Le Mahjong

Less Fuss. More Mahjong.

Your iPhone companion for mahjong with real tiles and real people. Keep score, calculate payouts, follow seat and round winds, and keep the rules close at hand. Between game nights, play Character Quiz, Faan Quiz and Mahjong Quest.

At the table

Start a Local session to keep score on one phone, or invite friends to a Shared session with a four-letter code. The host records each hand, while joined players follow the same scores and history on their own phones. Players without the app can be included too.

Choose the starting dealer, then turn the phone sideways for the play screen. Dealer and round winds advance with recorded results. Swipe to scores, player statistics and hand history, open House Rules, or tap or shake to roll the three-dimensional dice. Finished sessions return to portrait results.

Between game nights

Character Quiz builds quick tile recognition. Faan Quiz challenges you to score winning hands and explains each answer. Mahjong Quest puts you against a computer rival in short tile duels, with six tables to unlock and 18 medals to earn.

House Rules covers Hong Kong Traditional with Le Mahjong Club's house scoring rules, illustrated hands and adjustable payout settings. Characters explains the tiles, with bundled Cantonese and Mandarin pronunciation audio.

Common questions

Do I need an account or the internet? No account is required. Local sessions, practice games, rules and pronunciation audio work offline. Shared sessions require internet to sync your table's records.

Why does my seat move after joining? The table turns to your perspective so your own seat is at the bottom. Everyone keeps the same physical seat and wind.

Can someone join halfway through a Shared game? A new player can request an occupied seat. The host approves the replacement; the new player uses their own name and phone while retaining that seat's score.

Why did a session end? A session ends automatically after more than 12 hours without a recorded hand. Hosts also receive an inactivity prompt after two hours. Ending a session preserves its recorded results.

The app stopped opening. TestFlight builds expire 90 days after upload. Open TestFlight and install the latest available build.

I cannot hear the dice or pronunciation audio. Check your iPhone's silent mode and volume. Practice games also have an in-app sound control.
