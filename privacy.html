Privacy Policy

Le Mahjong Club for iPhone · Effective 27 October 2026

Le Mahjong Club does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services.

What the app stores

The app remembers a few settings on your device only, such as the current table wind and seat positions, so they are still there when you reopen it. This information never leaves your phone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network access

The app does not connect to the internet. All rules, tables and artwork are built into the app.

TestFlight

If you install the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and the feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy. The app itself adds nothing to this.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
